Codifies the group-named "Friz Special" as a defined opportunistic play —
a mean-reversion short-vol premium harvest — and extends the options
doctrine gate from three qualifications to four.

The play: after an extended multi-day run (~25%+ in 3-5 sessions) with
elevated IV, sell fully-covered far-OTM (~50%+) long-dated (6mo+) calls.
The vega does more work than the delta. Horizon 3-4 months. Buy back at
~30% of the credit received; do not hold for the remaining decay.

Default abandon rules (confirm or adjust per trade, in writing at entry):
hard stop at 1.5x entry credit; mandatory close-or-decide when the
underlying reaches 85% of strike; 90-day re-underwrite checkpoint;
written per-event decision for binaries in the window; never roll to
defend. Assignment at strike+premium is the failure fallback, so the
strike must be a livable sale price.
